how it works

A paint party,
delivered to your door.

We're a small mobile studio based on the Bellarine. We pack up everything you need and turn your living room, backyard or function space into the studio for the night.

01

Pick a painting

Choose from our catalogue of crowd-favourites, or tell us your theme and we'll design something custom — Taylor Swift era? French Riviera? Black cat under the moon? Done.

02

We bring it all

Canvases, easels, paints, brushes, aprons, tablecloths — the whole studio packs into the van and unpacks at your place. You do not have to lift a paintbrush ahead of time.

03

BYO & sip

Bring whatever you're drinking — bubbles, longnecks, lemon-lime & bitters, all welcome. We guide you through, stroke by stroke, for 2–3 messy, brilliant hours.
